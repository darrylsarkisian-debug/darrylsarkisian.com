This is a snapshot, not a live feed. No free API publishes real-time, state-level presidential approval — the two trackers that actually estimate this (Civiqs and Morning Consult) require paid access to their underlying data. These numbers are a one-time snapshot compiled by World Population Review, aggregating Civiqs' state-level tracking with The Economist/YouGov modeled state estimates, as polled in March 2026. Both sources model state-level results from national polling trends adjusted for state demographics and voting history, rather than from large independent polls in every state — treat this as a reasonable estimate, not precision polling, and expect it to be out of date by the time you're reading this.
